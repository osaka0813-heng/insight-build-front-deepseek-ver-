# Build009.4 — Process Evolution

## Objective
Turn World Processes into living systems that change over time rather than static topic pages.

## What is new
- EvolutionStage and EvolutionImpact data models.
- Localized evolution events for every seeded World Process.
- A dedicated Process Evolution screen with current stage, timeline, evidence impact and implications.
- Evidence can support, update or challenge the existing process thesis.
- Current-stage summary and Observe Next indicators on each World Process.
- Evolution navigation preserves the underlying reading session and overlay stack.

## Product progression
- Build009.1: Insights belong to World Processes.
- Build009.2: My World remembers and follows them.
- Build009.3: Related Process Engine connects them across domains.
- Build009.4: Processes evolve through stages as evidence changes.

## Validation
TypeScript/TSX files were syntax-transpiled with TypeScript. Full Expo runtime validation still requires importing the project into Snack or a local Expo environment.
