# Build010.3 — No New Global Insight Experience

Build010.3 completes the user-facing experience for days when the Daily Decision Engine does not detect a material world-level update.

## What changed

- Added a dedicated `NoNewGlobalInsightScreen`.
- Added a 2026-08-01 no-material-update daily decision generated by the rule engine.
- Shows what changed today without pretending it is a new Insight.
- Explains why the publication threshold was not crossed.
- Keeps the active World Process visible.
- Adds localized Observe Next items.
- Links back to the last meaningful Insight.
- Preserves right-swipe to World Process and left-swipe to History.
- Lets the final page of the previous Insight return to today's no-new landing.

## Daily loop behavior

- `publish_new`: open the new six-page Insight.
- `update_living`: open the updated six-page Insight.
- `no_new_global_insight`: open the no-new landing instead of recycling yesterday's Insight as today's content.
