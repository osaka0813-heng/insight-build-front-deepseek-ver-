# Build009.4 UX Revision — 2026-08-01

This revision addresses deep-experience feedback without changing the Process Evolution model.

## Reading surface
- The cover status now presents the editorial label “Today’s Observe” rather than the internal publication state “Update Living.”
- The persistent My World link has been removed from the top-left corner of every reading page.
- My World remains available in the final-page navigation bar.
- A horizontal right-swipe gesture opens My World from the reading surface. The gesture requires a clear horizontal movement to avoid interfering with vertical page navigation.

## Living Insight copy
- The previous observation is no longer repeated verbatim across the cover, question page and explorer sheet.
- The question lead now explains how the new evidence updates the previous Insight instead of repeating it.

## Signal explorer
- The signal root shows the signal, why it matters and its evidence.
- The separate root-level Sources section has been removed.
- Sources now appear only inside the Evidence detail, where they support the claim directly.
- The three current signals each include three sources where available, mixing event reporting with primary or contextual documents.

## Structure page refinement

- Removed “Explore” from the Before card.
- Removed “Explore” from the Now card.
- Kept “Explore” only on the central Shift card to avoid repetitive detail views.
